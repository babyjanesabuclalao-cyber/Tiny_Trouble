DUSTIN

Dustin Henderson — Science Lover & Problem Solver

Dustin Henderson is one of the original members of the Party and is known for his intelligence, humor, and love of science. His curiosity and problem-solving skills often help the group understand the strange events happening around Hawkins. Dustin also develops a close friendship with Steve Harrington and forms a strong bond with Eddie Munson.
