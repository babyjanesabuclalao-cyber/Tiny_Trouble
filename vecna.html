VECNA

Vecna — Formerly Henry Creel / Subject One

Vecna is a powerful supernatural being and one of the main threats connected to the Upside Down. He was once Henry Creel, who became Subject One at Hawkins National Laboratory. After gaining control of his abilities, he became Vecna and began targeting people in Hawkins.
