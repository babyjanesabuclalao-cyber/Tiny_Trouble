MAX

Max Mayfield — Skater & Arcade Ace

Max Mayfield is a skateboarder and arcade-game whiz who arrives in Hawkins during Season 2 and eventually becomes part of the Party. Independent and fearless, Max helps her friends face the supernatural threats surrounding Hawkins. She becomes especially close to Lucas Sinclair and forms a strong friendship with Eleven.
