STEVE

Steve Harrington — Protector & Ultimate Babysitter

Steve Harrington begins as one of Hawkins High School's popular students but gradually becomes a dependable protector and an important member of the Hawkins crew. He develops a close friendship with Dustin Henderson and later becomes close friends with Robin Buckley. Steve repeatedly joins the fight against the supernatural threats threatening Hawkins.
