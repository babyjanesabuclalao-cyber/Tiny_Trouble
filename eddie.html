EDDIE

Eddie Munson — Hellfire Club President & Dungeon Master

Eddie Munson is the metal-loving leader and Dungeon Master of Hawkins High School's Hellfire Club. Known for his rebellious personality and love of heavy metal and Dungeons & Dragons, Eddie becomes involved in the supernatural events of Hawkins after Chrissy Cunningham's death. He eventually joins Dustin and the others in their fight against the Upside Down and sacrifices himself to protect Dustin from the Demobats.
