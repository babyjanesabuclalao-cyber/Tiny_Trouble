Will Byers — Artist & Survivor

Will Byers is a creative and sensitive member of the Party whose disappearance becomes one of the first major mysteries of Hawkins. After being trapped in the Upside Down, Will returns home but remains connected to the supernatural world. His experiences continue to affect him as he and his friends face new threats from the Upside Down.
