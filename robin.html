Robin Buckley — Music Lover & Ally

Robin Buckley is a smart, witty, and resourceful teenager who becomes an important member of the Hawkins group. She works with Steve at Scoops Ahoy and later becomes involved in the fight against supernatural threats. Robin's intelligence, language skills, and quick thinking often help her friends solve difficult problems.
