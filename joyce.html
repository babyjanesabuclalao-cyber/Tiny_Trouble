Joyce Byers — Mother & Survivor

Joyce Byers is a caring and determined mother who refuses to ignore the strange events happening around her family. When her son Will disappears, she becomes determined to find him and discovers the supernatural forces connected to Hawkins. Joyce continues to protect her family and friends while facing dangerous threats from the Upside Down.
