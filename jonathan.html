JONATHAN

Jonathan Byers — Photographer

Jonathan Byers is a quiet and caring teenager who is especially protective of his family. He is passionate about photography and becomes involved in the strange events happening around Hawkins while searching for his missing brother Will. Jonathan continues to help his family and friends fight the supernatural threats connected to the Upside Down.
