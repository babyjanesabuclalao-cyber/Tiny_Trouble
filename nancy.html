Nancy Wheeler — Student Journalist & Investigator

Nancy Wheeler is a determined and brave student who investigates the strange events happening in Hawkins. After Barb's disappearance, Nancy becomes involved in uncovering the truth behind the supernatural events surrounding the town. She is observant, independent, and willing to face danger to protect the people she cares about.
