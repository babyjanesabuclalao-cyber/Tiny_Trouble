ABOUT THE SERIES

WHAT IS STRANGER THINGS?

Stranger Things is an American science-fiction horror television series created by Matt Duffer and Ross Duffer, also known as the Duffer Brothers.

The story takes place mainly in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana during the 1980s. It follows a group of young friends who become involved in mysterious events after the disappearance of their friend Will Byers.

Their search leads them to Eleven, a mysterious girl with supernatural abilities, and eventually to a terrifying alternate dimension called the Upside Down.

WHY STRANGER THINGS?

FRIENDSHIP. MYSTERY. COURAGE.

One of the main ideas of Stranger Things is the importance of friendship and staying together during difficult times. The characters face monsters, mysteries, loss, and danger while continuing to help and protect one another.

The series also became known for its 1980s setting, memorable characters, supernatural story, music, visual style, and mixture of horror, science fiction, adventure, comedy, and drama.

From the quiet streets of Hawkins to the terrifying Upside Down, Stranger Things creates a world where ordinary friendships meet extraordinary dangers.
