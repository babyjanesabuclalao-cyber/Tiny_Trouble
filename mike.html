MIKE

Mike Wheeler — Leader of the Party

Mike Wheeler is one of the central members and the leader of the Party. A loyal and determined friend, Mike becomes deeply involved in the search for Will Byers and later helps Eleven and his friends fight the supernatural threats coming from the Upside Down. He is also a passionate Dungeons & Dragons player and is romantically involved with Eleven.
