HAWKINS NATIONAL LABORATORY / SUBJECT 011

ELEVEN

Jane Hopper — Formerly Jane Ives

Eleven is a young girl with powerful psychic abilities who was raised and experimented on at Hawkins National Laboratory by Dr. Martin Brenner. After escaping the laboratory, she becomes close friends with Mike Wheeler, Lucas Sinclair, Dustin Henderson, and Will Byers. She later becomes the adopted daughter of Jim Hopper and one of the central figures in the fight against the Upside Down and its supernatural threats.

REAL NAME Jane Ives
ALIAS Eleven / El / Jane Hopper
