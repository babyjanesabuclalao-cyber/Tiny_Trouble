Jim Hopper — Hawkins Police Chief

Jim Hopper is the chief of police in Hawkins and one of the town's main protectors. He investigates strange cases and becomes involved in the supernatural events connected to the Upside Down. Hopper is tough and determined, but he deeply cares about the people close to him, especially Eleven and Joyce.
