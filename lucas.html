LUCAS

Lucas Sinclair — Pragmatic Friend & Ranger

Lucas Sinclair is one of the original members of the Party and is known for his practical thinking, determination, and courage. Although he is initially skeptical of Eleven, he eventually becomes one of her close friends. Lucas later develops a relationship with Max Mayfield while continuing to stand beside his friends as they face the supernatural threats in Hawkins.
